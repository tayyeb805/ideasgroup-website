Frequently Asked Questions

Why IDEAS One & IDEAS Group?

Clear answers regarding location, apartment layouts, commercial spaces, modern amenities, and construction status.

IDEAS One offers direct access to G.T Road at B-17 Gate 2, Islamabad — a premium commercial and residential spot. It is a CDA approved project with 20+ modern amenities, situated in a sector recording 21% year-on-year growth (Source: Zameen Property Index 26).
Located directly at Gate 2, Sector B-17, Islamabad, on Main G.T Road. It is strategically connected: Margalla Avenue (1 min), Motorway M-1 (5 min), CPEC Route (5 min), Islamabad Airport (15 mins), and Hazara Motorway (15 min drive).
Residences cater to diverse family requirements: Studio Apartments (450 sq.ft), 1 Bedroom Apartments (720 sq.ft), 2 Bedroom Apartments (1,275 sq.ft & 1,450 sq.ft), and spacious 3 Bedroom Luxury Suites (2,000 sq.ft).
Retail shops, corporate offices, food & beverage outlets, and a departmental store in a high-footfall location. Commercial units range from 153 sq.ft to 343 sq.ft. The project brings 236+ commercial shops and a 130+ unit food court into one address.
World-class amenities include 24/7 security with CCTV, smart building management (BMS), high-speed elevators, dedicated and guest parking, rooftop 360° restaurant, indoor swimming pool, spa & sauna, fitness center, scenic 2 lake views, 100% backup power, modern escalators, food court, 24/7 on-site maintenance, kids play area, day care, departmental store, automated fire fighting, smart RFID card access, and EV charging stations.
Over 8,000+ families are already residing in this rapidly expanding community. The area has demonstrated up to 21% annual property growth (Source: Multi Gardens B-17 Islamabad, Zameen Property Index), outstanding highway connectivity, an emerging commercial ecosystem, and high potential for long-term ROI.
Construction work is currently in progress at IDEAS One. 80% of basement work has been completed. Selected commercial spaces have been handed over to leading brands, and mall operations have partially started.
