Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding booking procedures, installment milestones, and official ownership guarantees.

Are IDEAS ONE and IDEAS Tower CDA approved? +
Yes, both IDEAS ONE and IDEAS Tower are fully vetted and approved under the regulatory framework of the Capital Development Authority (CDA) and Multi Gardens (MPCHS) Sector B-17 guidelines.
How does the 10% Cash Lump-Sum Discount work? +
Investors who choose to make full 100% payment at the time of booking receive an automatic 10% discount on the total unit price, saving hundreds of thousands or millions of rupees upfront.
Can Overseas Pakistanis book units and pay online? +
Yes, Overseas Pakistanis can complete the entire booking online via official direct bank wire transfers to IDEAS Group company accounts. Official digital booking receipts and payment statements are issued promptly.
What are the possession timelines for both projects? +
IDEAS Tower is currently over 90% completed with possession handover scheduled on the 18-month to 30-month timeline. IDEAS ONE follows a comprehensive 60-month milestone schedule aligned with construction stages.
Are there any hidden escalation or transfer charges? +
No hidden charges. Per-square-foot rates are fixed upon booking. Utility, connection, and official government transfer fees are transparently outlined as per standard society and regulatory bylaws.
Can I customize my installment schedule? +
Yes, our sales and financial advisory team at the site office can accommodate customized balloon or quarterly installment structures tailored to your cash flow requirements.
